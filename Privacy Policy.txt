FocusGuard Privacy Policy
Updated: 2026-10-23

What FocusGuard reads

The desktop app reads the name and window title of the app you're using. The
browser extension reads your active tab's full URL and title when you switch
tabs or a page finishes loading. It skips internal browser and extension pages.

The extension sends this information to FocusGuard on your computer at
http://localhost:9001. It can do this outside a study session if the desktop
server is still running. Disable the extension to stop it reading your tabs.

FocusGuard checks this activity against your goal and rules. AI requests go to
local Ollama at 127.0.0.1:11434. There are no screenshots, analytics or telemetry.

What's saved on your computer

Session history is saved in agent/logs/. These files contain full URLs, page
titles, timestamps and process names, along with classification scores and
reasons. They also record when an activity was blocked. Settings and background
rules are saved in agent/config/settings.json. Console output can also include
your goals, window titles and URLs, so saving that output creates another copy
of those details.

The extension keeps the latest blocked URL, title and reason in your browser's
local extension storage. It uses this to display the block page. The record
stays there until another block replaces it or you clear it.

These records aren't encrypted by FocusGuard and aren't deleted automatically.
URLs can contain private document paths, account details or access tokens.
Anyone with access to the relevant files or browser profile may be able to read
them. Your backups, browser and Ollama installation may keep their own copies
or records, which you'll need to manage separately.

When an internet connection is used

Setup downloads Python packages, Ollama and the models. Visiting the add-on
store and downloading extension updates also use the internet. Those services
have their own privacy policies.

Activity classification happens locally. FocusGuard's AI client ignores remote
Ollama host and HTTP proxy environment settings. FocusGuard doesn't upload your
activity logs to the project maintainer.

Deleting your data

Exit FocusGuard through its tray menu before deleting the files in agent/logs/
and any console or debug output you've saved. To reset your settings, delete
agent/config/settings.json and run setup again before using the app.

Clear the extension's data in your browser or uninstall it to remove the saved
block record. You can turn off automatic launches in FocusGuard's Settings.
Deleting FocusGuard's files doesn't remove copies in backups or Ollama's data.

Questions or concerns

You can ask general privacy questions in the GitHub issue tracker. Please leave
out personal activity and remove private details from any logs you share.

For a security issue, use GitHub's private "Report a vulnerability" option if
it's available. Otherwise, ask for a private contact channel without posting
exploit details or personal data.
